# StockSync Desktop

**Smart supplier spreadsheet reconciliation for Windows.**

StockSync Desktop is a planned Windows application designed to simplify the process of comparing supplier spreadsheets against existing product catalogues.

Businesses often spend hours manually checking supplier price lists, updating product information, identifying discontinued products, correcting mismatched SKUs and managing stock changes. StockSync aims to automate this process through intelligent product matching, data validation and automated comparison tools.

## Our Vision

Our goal is to create a powerful yet easy-to-use desktop application that helps businesses manage supplier updates efficiently, accurately and without unnecessary complexity.

StockSync Desktop aims to provide:

- Intelligent product matching across different supplier formats
- Automatic detection of price and stock changes
- Identification of new, missing and discontinued products
- Duplicate detection and catalogue cleaning
- Supplier-specific configurations and remembered mappings
- Price anomaly detection to identify potentially incorrect data
- Review and approval workflows for detected changes
- Clean, export-ready product catalogues
- Historical comparison and reporting
- Offline-first functionality with a focus on data privacy

## Designed for Businesses

StockSync is intended for small businesses, independent retailers, online stores, wholesalers and anyone who regularly works with supplier spreadsheets and product catalogues.

Whether you're managing hundreds or thousands of products, our goal is to make supplier updates faster, easier and more reliable.

## Privacy & Data Ownership

StockSync Desktop is being designed with a local-first approach. Our goal is to keep business data, supplier files and product catalogues on the user's own computer rather than requiring cloud uploads or subscriptions.(I am heavy on privacy so no data collection on StockSync)
